# Hi, I'm Josh Powell.

I've been doing IT work for more than 15 years. I've handled the hands-on stuff, the corporate systems work, and plenty of problems that sat between the two.

Some days it was replacing a motherboard in an executive's laptop. Some nights it was keeping ISP towers running. Later it was pharmacy go-lives, data-center migrations, healthcare and pharmacy EDI, and master data that had to line up across systems.

I've run my own IT consulting business and supported networks and desktops. In corporate systems, I worked with SAP, SharePoint, EDI pipelines, vendors, and day-to-day operations when they stopped agreeing.

My master-data work covered worldwide sales, vendor, employee, shipping, customer, and material records, including more than 1,200 parts. I also worked with integrations across SAP instances and the SharePoint pages supporting those processes.

## How I work

I fix the problem in front of me first and work backwards from there. Then I look at what allowed it to happen to keep it from happening again.

If the process still relies on a hidden spreadsheet, unclear ownership, or one person knowing every exception, the problem isn't fixed. It is waiting to come back.

My methodology:

- Test assumptions before building around them.
- Write down what changed in plain language.
- Make the answer work for the people using it.
- Say when something failed.
